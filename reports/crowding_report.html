⭐ The method (grounded in the ticketing data)

For each route + hour we place the month's mobile-ticket boardings and alightings on the route's GTFS stop sequence and walk it to get the on-board load:

load(after stop i) = Σj≤i boardings(j) − Σj≤i alightings(j)

Because this dataset records both boarding and alighting stops, load is computed directly — no alighting estimation. We then convert to a per-vehicle figure for an average bus in that hour and compare to capacity:

per_vehicle_load = load / COVERAGE / trips_month  ·  occupancy = per_vehicle_load / CAPACITY

Assumptions (documented, tunable): COVERAGE = 4.5% (mobile share), CAPACITY = 60 pax/bus, trips from the GTFS schedule × 31 days. Levels: Low <0.5, Medium 0.5–0.8, High 0.8–1.0, Very High >1.0 occupancy, shown to riders as seats available, standing room, crowded, packed. The estimate is most reliable as a relative ordering of crowding — which the planner needs.

Day types: each route-hour's monthly profile is scaled to weekdays, Saturdays and Sundays/holidays by the B2 demand model (b2), which beat the monthly average by 27% on a held-out week (src/ml/compare_models.py). A route-hour is low-confidence with <2 scheduled buses/day or <30 ticketed passengers in the month.

1 · Validation — does it match reality?

Checks against known patterns (commute peaks, busy corridors, weekday vs weekend) and the accounting itself:

⑤ Accounting is consistent: alightings / boardings = 1.0 (≈1.0 → load returns to zero by the terminus), and 98.7% of trips board before they alight along the sequence.

5 · Using it — the rider's question

The model answers "will my bus be crowded?" per route, hour, and trip leg:

python src/query_crowding.py --route 623DOWN --hour 18
python src/query_crowding.py --route 623DOWN --hour 18 --from "Munirka Village" --to "Nehru Place Terminal"

Outputs the crowding level + the load profile along the route (and the worst segment on your leg). The app's planner (src/api/) uses the same tables: direct trips and trips with one change, ranked on time vs crowding.

6 · Caveats

  • Coverage ~4.5%: mobile ticketing is a sample; absolute per-vehicle numbers depend on the COVERAGE constant. Treat levels as relative; anchor on ETM when it arrives.
  • Sparse route-hours: per-vehicle scaling divides by scheduled trips/hour, and few tickets make a noisy profile; route-hours with <2 scheduled buses/day or <30 ticketed passengers in the month (12,129 of 20,739) are flagged low-confidence and kept out of headline stats.
  • Stop resolution: ~2% of ticket stops are unresolved and excluded; 11% of tickets drop out overall (unresolved stop or stop not on the GTFS sequence).
  • Capacity constant (60): not split by bus type / AC yet.
